Amazon, which has committed two hundred twenty billion dollars to spending this year, disclosed that it had spent more cash than it earned. Reddit shares dropped twenty-nine per cent on weaker user figures. The five largest technology firms have committed eight hundred billion dollars to A I investment in twenty twenty-six, and industry-wide outlays are approaching one trillion dollars. November's earnings reports will show whether the returns are arriving.

Golden State won the opener and the fourth and fifth games, while Atlanta took the second, the third, and the sixth game on Thursday to force a decider.
