With the US midterm elections less than a month away, Wall Street is bracing for stocks to fall if Democrats regain control of both houses of Congress, Bloomberg reported.

According to Bloomberg, voters appear likely to hand the party a majority in at least the House of Representatives. The outlook for the Senate is far less certain, the outlet said, leaving control of Congress unresolved going into the vote.

Bloomberg's report, published on 22 October under the headline "US Midterm Elections 2026: What Results Could Mean for Wall Street," assessed the market implications of the possible electoral outcomes.

Separately, MarketWatch reported in its Market Pulse coverage that European stocks opened lower after a weak finish on Wall Street and computer outages.
