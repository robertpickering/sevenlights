The US midterm election is less than a month away, and Wall Street is bracing for stocks to fall if Democrats regain control of both houses of Congress, Bloomberg reported. Voters appear likely to hand the party a majority in at least the House of Representatives, the outlet said.

The outlook for the Senate is far less certain, Bloomberg reported.

Separately, European stocks opened lower after a weak Wall Street finish and computer outages, MarketWatch reported.
