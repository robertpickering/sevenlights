Arab parties could hold a critical balance of power in Israel's election, the BBC reported.

According to the BBC, disaffection has often driven down turnout among Arab Israelis, but this time there is a strong push to get rid of the right-wing governing coalition.

The Economist published an article arguing that Israel's Arab parties could be the kingmakers in its election.
