Regal will release all six episodes of "Heated Rivalry" in more than 650 theaters nationwide for a limited run from November 6 to 30, Variety reported. The outlet notes the release comes almost a year after the queer hockey romance series premiered in the US.

The theatrical presentations pair episodes two at a time, per Variety's report on the release. The Hollywood Reporter says special marathon screenings will take place at cinemas around the world, including at the US chains Regal, AMC and Cinemark.

The Hollywood Reporter confirmed the theatrical release of the series.
