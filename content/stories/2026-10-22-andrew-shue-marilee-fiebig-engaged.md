Andrew Shue and Marilee Fiebig are engaged, according to The Shade Room, which identifies them as the ex-spouses of Amy Robach and T.J. Holmes. Hollywood Life reports that Shue, the "Melrose Place" star, proposed to Fiebig, described as his girlfriend of nearly three years; a separate Hollywood Life piece identifies Fiebig as Holmes' ex.

The Shade Room describes the couple as having come together after major life changes, framing the engagement as an unexpected chapter for both.

Hollywood Life reports that Robach and Holmes, former "GMA3" co-anchors, lost their jobs at ABC News following an off-screen romance between the two.
