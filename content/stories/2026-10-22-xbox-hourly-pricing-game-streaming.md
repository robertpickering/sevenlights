Ars Technica reported that Xbox wants to charge users 24 to 40 cents per hour to stream games they have already bought.

The pricing described in the report is billed by the hour, with the 24-to-40-cent range given in the report's headline.

The report appeared in October 2026 in Ars Technica's gaming section.
