Kimi Antonelli won the United States Grand Prix from pole position at the Circuit of the Americas on Sunday, a dominant drive that left the 20-year-old Mercedes driver two points short of his first world championship.

Antonelli led from the start, held to a one-stop strategy while rivals went aggressive, and took his ninth career victory. Charles Leclerc finished second for Ferrari after climbing from fourth on the grid, and Max Verstappen third for Red Bull.

The title was not decided in Austin because Antonelli needed team-mate George Russell to finish ninth or lower and Lewis Hamilton fifth or lower; they crossed the line eighth and fourth. Russell served a five-second penalty for a first-lap infringement while passing Oscar Piastri. Hamilton closed to within a point of Russell in the drivers' standings. A ninth-place finish in Mexico City next weekend would secure the championship for Antonelli regardless of Russell's result, and make him the youngest champion in the sport's history.

Verstappen, second on the grid, saved soft tyres for the race and tried an early two-stop, but reported engine-braking inconsistency and radioed that the car was "undriveable" before a second stop on lap 32 dropped him behind the leaders. He recovered past Lando Norris late to take third. Leclerc took second on fresher tyres in the closing laps.

Norris, fifth on a one-stop that briefly held up the pack, has now gone four races without a podium, and Piastri, the reigning champion, was seventh as McLaren conceded it was "just not quick enough" in Austin. Isack Hadjar's Red Bull ran competitively before dropping behind.

Valtteri Bottas's engine failure on lap 52 brought out a late virtual safety car that neutralised Hamilton's charge at Verstappen for the final podium place. Oliver Bearman and Franco Colapinto also retired.

Antonelli, who took his first pole since the Belgian Grand Prix, was never seriously threatened once Verstappen's strategy failed, and Mercedes stuck to its plan "as long as you can," in the words of the team's instructions.

Mexico City decides next Sunday whether the drivers' title is settled there or carried to the season's remaining races.
