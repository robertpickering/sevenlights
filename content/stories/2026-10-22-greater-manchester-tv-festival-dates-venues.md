The TV Festival, previously known as the Edinburgh TV Festival, has unveiled the first details of its inaugural event in Greater Manchester, Variety reported. The festival is set to be held in the northern city for the first time next year, keeping its four-day schedule while taking place later in the month than usual.

Deadline reports the festival will move one week back from its traditional Edinburgh slot. Deadline describes the event as the UK's biggest TV Festival.

Sony international productions boss Wayne Garvie joins the festival as advisory chair, Deadline reported.
