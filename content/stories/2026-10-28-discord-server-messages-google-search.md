Discord will open its largest public communities to web search, publishing pages that carry server descriptions and discussion summaries from Nov. 16 and beginning to show individual messages word-for-word in Google and other search results from mid-December.

The change applies only to so-called Discoverable Servers — communities of at least 1,000 members, at least eight weeks old, that anyone may join without an invitation and that meet Discord's activity and safety rules. More than 63,000 servers qualify. Discord said the aim is to make useful answers easier to find without first searching inside the platform, since knowledge in gaming guides, support discussions and community forums often stays hard for outsiders to reach. Each qualifying server will receive a Server Web Page; where moderators publish none, Discord will create a default page. Private servers, direct messages and group messages are excluded, and no message history predating the rollout will be indexed, the company said.

Usernames and avatars will not appear beside the quoted messages, Engadget reported. Server administrators may exclude channels from the web pages, and individual users may disable the listing of their message content through the Data and Privacy section of account settings. Discord cautioned that search engines may retain indexed or cached copies after removal, requiring separate steps at the engines themselves. Mashable noted that opting out of message display does not remove channel summaries, which have separate administrative controls.

Discord said the feature would not apply in select markets, including the EU, the UK, Canada, Brazil and Australia.

The company framed the pages as a way for communities to be found, but the indexing of message text carries privacy weight: discussions once visible only to server members can surface to the open web, searchable by employers, journalists or strangers. Critics of the design noted the default is inclusion, with individuals required to opt out account-wide.

Server administrators' opt-out controls are available now. The message listings will roll out gradually through December, with pages covering only discussions from Nov. 16 onward.
